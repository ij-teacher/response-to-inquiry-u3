LESSON 02 · U3-2

Replying with a quotation

Read product prices, calculate a discount and explain payment terms.

Read the business letter

Subject: Quotation for Canned Food Products

Dear Purchasing Assistant,

Thank you for your inquiry regarding our canned food products. We are pleased to provide a quotation based on your request for canned fish, canned fruit, and canned vegetables.

Terms and Conditions

Payment Terms: 50% deposit, balance upon delivery*

Sincerely,

(Signature) Sales Representative Sales Representative Pacific Harvest Foods Co.

*

*pay half the total price upfront, then pay the remaining half when the goods are delivered.

Deposit (訂金): Money you pay in advance to confirm an order. It counts toward the total price.

Balance (餘款): The amount you still need to pay after paying the deposit.

*Subtotal (小計) is the amount before tax or additional charges.

Total (總計) is the final amount including tax and additional charges.

MONEY & MEANING

Understand the quotation.

Subtotal → discount → final total

$1,634.00 − $163.40 = $1,470.60

The subtotal is the sum of the six item amounts. This quotation deducts 10% because the order exceeds $1,500. No tax or shipping charge is specified.

50% deposit, balance upon delivery

$735.30 upfront + $735.30 on delivery

Deposit 訂金: an advance payment toward the purchase.
Balance 尾款: what remains to be paid.

Language tip: “How long does this quotation remain in effect?” = “How long is this quotation valid?”

Subtotal vs. total: A subtotal is an intermediate sum. A final total is the amount payable after applicable discounts, tax and charges. Their exact calculation depends on the quotation.
